# School Generative AI Policy

## Final Assignments

Students may use generative AI tools for brainstorming, outlining, grammar feedback, and code explanation when the instructor allows it in the assignment guide.

Students must not submit AI-generated work as their own original work. If generative AI tools are used, students must include a short disclosure that explains which tool was used and how it supported the work.

## Exams and Quizzes

Generative AI tools are not allowed during closed-book exams, timed quizzes, or assessments where external assistance is prohibited.

## Research Projects

For research projects, students may use generative AI to summarize background materials or generate search keywords. Students are responsible for checking sources, verifying claims, and citing original references.

## Instructor Overrides

Instructors may set stricter or more permissive rules for a specific course. Course-level instructions take priority over the general school policy.
