# School Generative AI Policy

## Final Assignments

Pikin dem fit use generative AI tools for brainstorming, outlining, grammar feedback, and code explanation when teacher allow am for assignment guide.

Pikin dem no suppose submit work wey AI generate as their own original work. If na generative AI tools pikin dem use, dem must put short talk wey explain which tool dem use and how e support the work.

## Exams and Quizzes

No fit use generative AI tools during closed-book exams, timed quizzes, or assessment wey no allow make person help from outside.

## Research Projects

For research projects, pikin dem fit use generative AI to summarize background materials or generate search keywords. Pikin dem responsible to check sources, confirm claims, and talk where dem get the original references from.

## Instructor Overrides

Teacher dem fit set stricter or softer rules for one specific course. Course-level instruction dey pass the general school policy.
